# Recording checklist: Weekend Review

**What it is:** a talk-over recording, about 20 minutes, 9 slides. No code-along, nothing new.

## Before you record

1. Open `big-ideas/slides.html`, press **Ctrl+Shift+R**, then **F** (fullscreen).
2. Press **P** for the presenter window on your second screen. The notes are your cues.
3. Record only the slides window. Press **T** to start the clock.
